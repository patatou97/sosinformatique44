What the app does

  • Protein goal

    A daily target and meals logged in seconds. Today’s bar tells you where you stand at a glance.

  • Injection calendar

    Weekly reminder and injection-site rotation, so you never use the same spot twice in a row.

  • Weight and how you feel

    Weigh-ins, appetite notes and side-effect notes on a single timeline.

  • Imperial or metric

    Pounds or kilograms, your choice. Designed first for the US and the UK.

SnapCal is a personal tracking tool. It does not replace medical advice or your treatment’s instructions.
