The game

  • One-touch controls

    Tap to jump, hold to jump higher. Nothing else to learn.

  • Five hand-crafted levels

    The countryside, the flooded highway, the city at dusk, the mountain road and the storm-hit relay station.

  • Bonus levels

    The underwater lake, the abandoned mine on a mine cart, the metro tunnels. If you can find them.

  • Bolts and a battery

    Collect bolts to repair XT-2’s battery. The 15 golden bolts unlock a secret ending.

  • Futuristic suits

    Dress up your robot: the NOVA suit and three color suits.

  • No account

    High scores stay on your phone. The game starts even without a connection, but it contains ads, which need the internet to show. In English and French.

FAQ

Do I need internet to play?

The game starts even without a connection. It contains ads, which need the internet to show; the “Remove ads” option removes them.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress, high scores and settings are stored on your phone only.

How do I unlock the secret ending?

Find the 15 golden bolts hidden in the levels.

I changed phones and my purchases are gone.

Open Menu, then Shop, and tap “Restore purchases” while signed in to the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase.

Is the game suitable for kids?

Violence is mild and cartoon-like, with no blood: a tank, drones, bumps. Expected ratings: 9+ on the App Store, PEGI 7 / ESRB Everyone 10+ on Google Play. The game shows ads and offers optional purchases.

How do I change my ad choice?

The “Privacy” link in the game menu reopens the consent form.
